# MISSION
THE ORDINARY COMPANY™ exists to introduce "unorthodox" innovation at the intersection of media, design, and technology. We provide robust visual infrastructure solutions that modernize production pipelines for global corporations. Through our **Ajax™** platform, **Ajax Connect™** Digital Asset Management (DAM), and our elite in-house design team, we enable partners to operate at the velocity of the modern tech landscape.

# VISION
We envision a future where media moves exponentially faster than the present. The emergence of Generative AI is catalyzing a shift in throughput and output that current systems are unprepared for. THE ORDINARY COMPANY is building the infrastructure necessary to manage this undeniable increase, empowering creative teams to utilize these innovations to design better, faster, and revolutionize the visual world around us.

# CORE VALUES
THE ORDINARY COMPANY is deeply rooted in the love of media, design, technology, science, humanity, and innovation. Every operational decision we make is an expression of this commitment. We believe that true progress occurs when technical rigor meets creative intuition.

# FOUNDING PRINCIPLES
1. **UNORTHODOX ARCHITECTURE:** We reject legacy workflows in favor of systems that prioritize speed and scalability without sacrificing aesthetic integrity.
2. **INFRASTRUCTURE AS A SERVICE:** High-fidelity visual output is not an accident; it is the result of well-engineered infrastructure.
3. **HUMAN-CENTRIC INNOVATION:** Technology, including AI, serves as an extension of human creativity, not a replacement for it.
4. **EXPONENTIAL THROUGHPUT:** We design for a world where the demand for visual content is infinite, and the capacity to create it must follow suit.
